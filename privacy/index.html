Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Terms of Service

This policy explains what information Starks Labs ("we", "us") handles when you use any of our apps, bots, or services, now and in the future (together, the "Apps"). Short version: we collect only what an App needs to work, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

1. What we collect

When you use an App on Discord, Discord shares some information with it. Depending on the App, that can include:

  • Discord identifiers: your user ID, username, and display name, plus the IDs of the server and channel where you used the App.
  • Messages and attachments you send to the App: direct messages to the bot, messages that mention it, commands you run, and files you attach.
  • Information you choose to enter into an App, such as tasks, schedules, notes, or budget entries.

We don't ask for your email address, phone number, password, or payment details, and we don't use cookies, ads, or tracking on the Apps.

2. How we use it

  • To run the App and reply to you.
  • To remember context from earlier conversations so the App is more useful to you.
  • To control who can use an App (for example, invite-only access lists).
  • To keep the Apps secure and fix problems.

3. AI processing

Some Apps send the content of your messages to an AI model to write a reply. That can be a model running on our own computers or a third-party AI provider such as Anthropic. When a third-party provider is used, your message is handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

4. Where it's stored

Information is stored on computers that Starks Labs runs and controls, in the United States. We don't run a public cloud database of user data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We only share it:

  • with service providers needed to run an App (such as Discord itself or an AI provider, as described above);
  • if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it to run the App, then delete it. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 8).

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, such as access limits and keeping services off the public internet where we can. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Access or delete: ask us what we have about you, or ask us to delete it, in the Starks Labs Discord server or by direct message to the App's bot account.
  • Stop sharing: stop messaging the App, or remove it from your server.

9. Children

The Apps are not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Reach us in the Starks Labs Discord server, or send a direct message to the App's bot account on Discord.
